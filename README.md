# ObzueAI QuantNET DNA

Simulated multi-layer financial desk. Accounts, wallet, swap, social, marketplace, NFT-style seals, launchpad, and a mining simulation.

This repository is the research and product record. It is **not** a clone of Ethereum, Optimism, Arbitrum Nitro, Lightning, Circle stablecoin contracts, OpenZeppelin, Slither, Qiskit, Cirq, or PennyLane.

## What the product actually does

- Email, Google, and X sign-in
- Personal, business, and corporate desk profiles with different sandbox fees
- Ledger balances in QDNA, QUSD, and QNET
- Sends between handles, swaps at a fixed mark, a public tape
- Posts, comments, follows
- Treasury listings and user-launched token, meme, and NFT records priced in QUSD
- A cooldown tick labeled as a simulation, not hashpower

Balances are application rows. They are not coins, deposits, or claims on ObzueAI. Do not send real assets to any label the desk shows.

## Layers inside this product

| Layer | Meaning here |
| --- | --- |
| L1 | Accounts, balances, ledger |
| L2 | Swaps, fees, tape |
| L3 | Social, market, launchpad, simulation tick |

These are modules in one website. They are not consensus, data availability, or a rollup.

## Explicitly out of scope

- No private keys or seed phrases
- No quantum custody and no claim that balances are quantum-safe
- No regulated KYC. The attestation flag is self-set. Do not store passports or national IDs here.
- No bank withdrawal and no public-chain deployment
